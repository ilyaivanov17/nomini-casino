## Slot guide: themes, volatility and comparison

Slots share a familiar format, but the details behind their features are not interchangeable. This guide explains how common mechanics are described and what to look for in a game's own rules. Provider data, RTP, volatility labels and feature availability can vary by version or market, so use the in-game help for the exact title you can access.

## Starburst

## Starburst: a quick guide

Starburst keeps things visually simple: bright gems, a clean layout and a quick rhythm. The details stored for this title list wild symbols and respins. It is easy to get a feel for the basic layout, but the in-game help is still the place to check the exact rules for the version on offer.

|---|---:|---|---|---|
| NetEnt | 96.1% | Low | 0.1–100 EUR | Wild symbols, Respins |

### What the figures do — and do not — tell you

For Starburst, the published RTP is a theoretical average across a large number of rounds, not a return forecast for a short session. Volatility describes variation in results; it cannot predict the next spin.

### What to check before playing

Before launching Starburst, read its in-game help and paytable. Confirm symbol values, feature triggers and any rules for free spins or jackpots. Set a spending limit first and follow local age and legal requirements.

### Volatility and variance

A volatility label is, at most, a broad guide when the provider explains what it means. It is not a promise of frequent small wins or rare large ones. Consider it alongside the actual rules and your own risk tolerance. A short personal sample cannot reliably reproduce the long-run distribution, and one unusual session should not be treated as proof of a general pattern.
