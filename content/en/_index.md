### How information is assessed

For game-related articles, we separate the rules of play from features that may appear only in particular versions. Where reliable game information is available, we explain paylines or other win mechanics, bonus rounds, volatility descriptions and the published return-to-player (RTP) figure. RTP describes a theoretical long-term average across a large volume of play. It does not forecast an individual result or session. Values can differ between configurations, so the in-game information for the version available to a reader is the relevant reference.

For promotions, the advertised amount is only one part of the offer. We look at the wagering base and multiplier, eligible titles, contribution rates, expiry dates, maximum stake, withdrawal limits and any restrictions on payment methods. Those conditions determine what a promotion actually requires. Terms can change between campaigns and may depend on country, account status or deposit route; the current offer terms published by the operator take precedence over this guide.

For payments and account access, we distinguish between a stated processing estimate and the time a particular transaction takes to complete. Identity checks, payment-provider reviews, banking cut-offs, local rules, fees and account-specific restrictions may all affect the result. A method appearing in a general list does not mean it is available to every account or country, and an estimated time is not a guarantee.
