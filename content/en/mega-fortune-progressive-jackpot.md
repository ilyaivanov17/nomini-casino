## The €3.5 million win: what the original announcement confirms

On 11 October 2018, NetEnt published an announcement about a €3.5 million Mega Fortune jackpot. The winner was playing at PokerStars Casino and was from Lower Saxony, Germany. NetEnt described him as a journalist who had arrived home from work and started a game session while a pizza was in the oven. The announcement also mentioned a deposit of just over €2 before the win. At the time, the company called it the largest online payout in PokerStars Casino history. NetEnt added that Mega Fortune had paid eight jackpots worth more than €1 million each during 2018.

One distinction is essential when retelling the story: **the announcement refers to a deposit of slightly more than €2, not a €2 stake on the spin that won the jackpot.** It does not disclose the stake used on that spin or how many rounds came before it. Saying that a €2 bet became €3.5 million would therefore turn the source's statement into a claim it does not support.

## How a progressive jackpot differs from a fixed prize

A fixed prize is set out in a game's paytable. A progressive jackpot works differently: its amount changes under a defined funding and calculation model until the trigger conditions are met and the prize is paid. Depending on the product, the pool may belong to one game instance or be shared across connected instances. The rules for the specific game should explain how the system works.

Before taking part, a player should be able to establish:

- where the jackpot contributions come from, whether there is a starting amount and whether a ceiling applies;
- which stake levels or activated paylines qualify for participation;
- what triggers the jackpot and how the pool is handled after a payout;
- whether the displayed RTP includes the jackpot or reports the base game and jackpot separately.

The UK Gambling Commission's technical guidance on progressive jackpots treats these points as part of a transparent rule set. It refers to funding, the starting seed, any ceiling and the way prizes are determined and awarded. This is a technical reference for the article, not a statement of the specific legal requirements that apply in Germany.

## What NetEnt lists for Mega Fortune

NetEnt's product information describes Mega Fortune as a five-reel, three-row slot with 25 paylines. It also lists Free Spins, Scatter symbols, Wild substitutions and a Jackpot Bonus Game containing three progressive jackpots: two smaller local prizes and one global jackpot.

Under the published game description, **three Bonus symbols on an activated payline** start the Jackpot Bonus Game. The feature uses three wheels. The player can stop a wheel, while arrows in the bonus sequence lead to further prize stages. The feature finishes with either a jackpot or a fixed coin prize. The product information does not establish that the precise timing of a click changes the chance of winning. The stop button should not be described as a skill technique or a way to make the jackpot happen on demand.

The public product page currently lists a theoretical RTP of 96.60%. However, the available information does not clearly assign a volatility category to the game. We therefore do not label it as “high” or “medium” volatility, and we do not calculate jackpot odds from the amount displayed. Details may differ between game versions, regional configurations and the help information available inside a particular game.

## Reading the 96.60% RTP figure in context

RTP means “return to player”. It is a theoretical percentage measured across a very large number of game rounds. A displayed RTP of 96.60% does not mean that someone who stakes €100 in a short session will receive €96.60 back. An individual session can end well above or below the theoretical long-term figure.

Progressive jackpots add another point to check: how is the jackpot component included in the RTP figure? The Gambling Commission notes that the base game and progressive jackpot may be monitored separately. The way RTP is presented should therefore make clear whether the figure combines both components or lists them separately.

The public Mega Fortune product page does not clearly say whether its 96.60% figure combines the base game and jackpot, or how those components are divided. For the version actually offered, the in-game help is the relevant source to consult.

## Jackpot size does not reveal volatility or probability

Volatility describes how widely and unevenly individual results can vary around a game's long-term expected return. Lower-volatility games tend to produce smaller wins more often; higher-volatility games may produce larger wins less frequently. A progressive jackpot is usually a rare event with the potential for a substantial payout. That alone does not establish the volatility category of the whole slot.

A large displayed jackpot is not evidence that a win is about to happen. The size of a possible prize and the probability of triggering it are separate facts. NetEnt's product page does not publish a specific probability for the Mega Fortune jackpot. There is consequently no reliable spin count, “best” stake or timing method that we can give as a way to improve the chance of winning.

## What the 2018 case can — and cannot — tell us

The payout illustrates the scale that a global progressive jackpot can reach. It does not show that a small deposit improves the odds, that a jackpot must be won after a certain period, or that the same result can be reproduced. The reported deposit of just over €2 also tells us nothing about the stake placed on the winning spin.

For a clearer picture of a progressive jackpot, look beyond the headline amount beside the game. Participation rules, trigger conditions, pool funding and the way the jackpot is represented in the RTP and in-game help are more useful for understanding the system than a single record-win story.

## Information to check before playing

Use the rules for the exact version being offered. Check whether the selected stake qualifies for the jackpot, whether particular paylines must be active, how the pool is reset after a win and whether the RTP figure includes the jackpot. Older screenshots and general game summaries may not describe the version currently available.

This article uses Mega Fortune as a historical and technical case study. It does not confirm that the game is currently available in Germany or permitted for every account. Availability and legal eligibility must be checked against the specific offer and the player's location. Gambling is not a way to earn income. Set a limit in advance, use only money you can afford to lose and do not try to recover losses by continuing to play.
